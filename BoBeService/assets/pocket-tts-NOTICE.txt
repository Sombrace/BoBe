Pocket TTS English pack for BoBe

Model: Pocket TTS, Copyright Kyutai, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Original: https://huggingface.co/kyutai/pocket-tts-without-voice-cloning
This pack uses the b6369a24 English generation, converted to Q8 GGUF. It is not
the later multilingual v3 model and does not provide voice cloning.

Converted weights, tokenizer and configuration are from davencyw's ptts test
layout at https://huggingface.co/davencyw/pocket-tts-ptts, immutable revision
7cc3c90b88f3e6617ded1b9b74100d1cd0162d90. This is a third-party conversion,
not an official Kyutai release. BoBe pins the SHA-256 of every downloaded file.

Voice sources (precomputed embeddings of the following recordings):
- Alba: Alba MacKenna, casual.wav, CC BY 4.0.
  https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-voices/blob/main/alba-mackenna/casual.wav
- Azelma, Eponine, Fantine: CSTR VCTK Corpus, CC BY 4.0, by Junichi
  Yamagishi, Christophe Veaux and Kirsten MacDonald, University of Edinburgh.
  https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3443
  Speakers p303, p262 and p244, sentence 023, mic1; enhanced by ai-coustics.
- Javert, Marius: Kyutai Unmute Voice Donation Project, CC0.
  Recordings Butter.wav and Selfie.wav, respectively.
  https://unmute.sh/voice-donation

Voice license documentation: https://huggingface.co/kyutai/tts-voices
CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Cosette (Expresso) and Jean (EARS) are deliberately excluded because their
source recordings are licensed for noncommercial use only.
